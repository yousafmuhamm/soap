# Image Credits

All imagery is sourced from **Unsplash** and **Pexels** (both free for commercial
use, no attribution required — source URLs logged here per master plan §2).

Selection rules applied: neutral/warm backgrounds only, no busy props, no visible
third-party logos or branded packaging, consistent warm colour grade, sourced at
≥2000px width.
