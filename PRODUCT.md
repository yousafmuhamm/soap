# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Wholesale skincare buyers, distributors, and prospective stockists evaluating whether Yeong Won belongs in their retail assortment. They need to understand the product, formulation, positioning, pack format, and brand credibility before making contact.

## Product Purpose

Present Yeong Won Stemcell Soap as a credible high-end wholesale proposition. The site should help qualified buyers understand the product quickly, trust its formulation and claims, and submit a stockist or wholesale enquiry.

## Brand Personality

High-end, elegant, refined, assured, and clinical. The voice should feel composed and knowledgeable, with luxury expressed through restraint and product detail rather than excess.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic beauty-store and conventional e-commerce styling, including crowded merchandising, discount-led language, trend-driven visual effects, and consumer checkout patterns that weaken the wholesale positioning.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with wholesale relevance and make the buyer's next step unmistakable.
2. Build confidence through precise product, formulation, packaging, and contact information.
3. Express luxury through controlled hierarchy, strong imagery, and deliberate restraint.
4. Keep clinical credibility grounded in clear, supportable skincare claims.
5. Remove consumer-shopping conventions that distract from stockist evaluation and enquiry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA as the minimum standard. Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic structure, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, and clear form feedback.
